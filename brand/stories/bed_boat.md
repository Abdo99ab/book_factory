{name} alone, eyes closed, calm. The duvet becomes the sea. The boat sails — the real bed and real pyjamas stay visible.

The adventure: three calm imaginative scenes in one — an island of pillows, a whale that yawns, a lighthouse that is the night-light. No monsters, no storms.

## Spread 10
A wake-up in the night. {name} opens eyes. The room is the room. The comfort object is there. 'The boat is still here. I am still the captain.' Eyes closed again.

## Spread 11
Second adventure scene, shorter and calmer.

## Spread 12
Morning light. The boat is a bed. {name} stayed in it.

## Spread 13
{carer} comes in. 'You sailed all night.' A real small celebration: choosing breakfast.

## Spread 14
